FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before getting started.

What is Smart Search?

Smart Search is an AI-powered search engine web app that combines Google results via SerpAPI with AI summaries, live suggestions, image search and video search in one clean interface.

Do I need an account to use it?

Yes — you can sign up with an email and password or log in instantly with your Google account. Your account unlocks search history, favorites, and profile settings.

What search modes are available?

Three: All (web results), Images, and Videos. Use the tabs above the results to switch between them instantly.

How do the AI answers work?

When you run a web search, Smart Search generates a concise AI direct answer at the top of the results, plus "People also ask" follow-ups and an AI chat for deeper questions.

Is Smart Search free to use?

Yes, it's free to get started. The app runs on a free SerpAPI plan, so there is a daily search limit, and the free hosting tier may sleep after inactivity.

Does it support dark mode?

Absolutely. Toggle between dark and light themes with one click — your preference is saved and restored on your next visit.

Is my search history private?

Your recent searches are stored locally in your browser, not on our servers, and you can clear them at any time.
